**Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** is an AI framework that combines **information retrieval** (searching a knowledge base) with **text generation** (LLMs) to produce more accurate, up-to-date, and verifiable responses.

---

### 1. The Core Problem: Why do we need RAG?
Standard Large Language Models (LLMs) have three fundamental limitations:
*   **Knowledge Cutoff:** They only know what was in their training data (e.g., "data up to January 2024").
*   **Hallucination:** They confidently generate plausible-sounding but factually incorrect answers.
*   **Black Box / No Citations:** You cannot easily verify *where* an answer came from.
*   **No Private Data:** They don't know your company's internal HR policy, your codebase, or your customer's support history.

**RAG solves this by "grounding" the LLM in external, verifiable data at inference time.**
